# The Thermodynamic Arrow

## A Boundary-Conditioned Corollary of Realized History and Scope

This repository contains an open Structural Flow investigation of the thermodynamic Arrow.

The central claim is deliberately conditional. The work does not derive thermodynamic asymmetry from Structural Flow, prescribe a unique future, or propose an additional physical mechanism that drives systems toward later states. Physics remains responsible for the asymmetric boundary conditions, thermodynamic variables, dynamics, realized processes, and particular outcomes.

The paper asks a narrower question: once a physically warranted thermodynamic history has actually been realized, what follows structurally from the fact that the history traversed that realized path?

Under the stated conditions, Post-Authorization Answerability supplies a no-free-reset constraint: continuation of the same realized history cannot simply erase an incorporated passage while retaining the same historical claim. This answerability is retrospective and conditional rather than a prospective rule selecting which future occurs.

The argument then separates a second burden: local thermodynamic orientations do not automatically establish one larger-scale Arrow. A singular Arrow at a declared scope requires both a warranted identification of the relevant local orientations as the same orientation and their joint support across that scope.

The resulting Arrow is therefore boundary-conditioned, history-conditioned, and scope-conditioned.

## What the claim does not establish

This work does not:

* explain why the universe has an asymmetric boundary;
* derive thermodynamic entropy or entropy increase;
* replace statistical mechanics;
* determine microscopic dynamics;
* prohibit fluctuations or accounted returns;
* require a unique successor;
* establish that every physical region shares one Arrow;
* or claim that every possible universe possesses the same temporal architecture.

The no-extra-driver claim is explicitly defeasible. A physically adequate same-scope case retaining the paper's stated conditions while nevertheless requiring a genuinely new irreducible Arrow-maintaining or future-driving job would defeat the corollary for that case.
